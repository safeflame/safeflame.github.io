System Flow

Alert in 100ms. Response in seconds.

SafeFlame AI doesn't just warn you — it acts. Here's the exact sequence from detection to resolution.

📡
Continuous Sensing
MQ2 gas sensor, dual flame detector, HX710B pressure sensor, and 50kg load cell read every loop cycle. Gas PPM, flame intensity, line pressure, and cylinder weight are all live.
⚡
Instant Response
Gas threshold crossed → SG90 servo closes the valve, both fans activate, buzzer sounds, WS2812B strip pulses red, DFPlayer plays the alarm siren.
📸
Photo Alert
ESP32 fetches a live JPEG from the ESP32-CAM and attaches it to a Telegram message and an SMS via SIM800L — so you see your kitchen immediately from your phone.
⏱️
Escalation
If unacknowledged after 5 minutes, full details and the photo are auto-sent to a backup contact. The dashboard shows a live countdown until escalation fires.
🎙️
Voice Control
"Hey Sentry, close the valve." INMP441 mic captures audio → ESP32 streams to wit.ai → intent matched → command executed → DFPlayer confirms with spoken response.
✅
All Clear
Gas drops below threshold → valve reopens (unless in Night Schedule or Away Mode) → fans return to auto → strip returns to green → Telegram and SMS send all-clear.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Does it need an internet connection to work?+

No. Core safety functions — valve shutoff, fan activation, buzzer, LED alerts — run entirely on the local ESP32 with no internet needed. SMS alerts also work via the SIM800L GSM module independently of WiFi. Internet is only required for Telegram alerts, the Gemini AI chat, and OTA firmware updates.

How is the gas valve controlled?+

An SG90 servo motor is mechanically coupled to a standard gas ball valve. When the ESP32 triggers a gas or flame alert, it drives the servo to the closed position in under a second. You can also open or close it manually from the dashboard, touchscreen, or voice command.

When will it ship?+

We're in final production preparations. Pre-booked orders will be prioritised and shipped in the order they're received once manufacturing is complete. You'll get an email update when your order is confirmed and again when it ships.

Does pre-booking require payment?+

No — pre-booking only reserves your place in the launch queue. Our team will contact you to arrange payment before dispatch.

What's the difference between the tiers?+

All three tiers include the full safety system: gas/flame/pressure sensors, auto valve shutoff, exhaust fans, LPG weight monitoring, Telegram/SMS alerts, web dashboard, and voice commands. The difference is display size (4" / 7" / 9"), camera count (1 / 2 / 4), and whether you get multi-room zone monitoring and the upgraded processor (Elite only).
